📱 Bet buttons opening the FanDuel website instead of the app?Every Bet button is a plain link to sportsbook.fanduel.com, which FanDuel’s own Apple file hands to the Sportsbook app. If yours opens in Safari, iOS has remembered the browser for that site. To fix it once: press and hold a Bet button and choose Open in “FanDuel” (or on the FanDuel web page, pull down and tap Open in the banner at the top). Calibrated from graded results once a bucket has 30+ legs.
  • Best single market per game kept; one leg per game, so no same-game correlation. N-leg parlay = top N not-yet-started legs by probability (optimal under independence).
